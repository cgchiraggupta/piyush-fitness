About Piyush

I'm Piyush Loharwadia, a fitness coach based in Dwarka, New Delhi. I spent 6+ years training at Chhatrasal Stadium across sprinting, long jump, high jump, and powerlifting — competing at district and national level before transitioning into coaching.

My powerlifting gold medals were earned through pure self-training — no coach, just discipline. That same mindset is what I bring to every client. I've coached 150+ clients in strength, HIIT, functional training, and athletic performance.

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced athlete — I'll build a program that actually works for you.
